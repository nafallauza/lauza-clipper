# Visual direction

Use `../brand.json` as the numeric source of truth. Palette approved by user;
Montserrat typography remains proposed. `../assets/typography-preview.png`
shows the proposed weights. Local OTF files and their OFL license are in
`../assets/fonts`; self-host the actual font files, and wait for font loading
before measuring captions or rendering.

## Typography and composition

Use ExtraBold for subtitles, Black for short punchlines, Bold for supporting
labels. Avoid all-caps paragraphs. Fit 2–5 words in at most two balanced lines;
avoid a lone short word on the second line when a balanced split is possible.
Keep the caption block's vertical center stable across one- and two-line cues.
Use off-white for normal words and a yellow active-word highlight with dark text.
Measure the chosen font; do not reuse Fira Sans widths after changing fonts.

Align repeated diagram headings and object baselines. Keep one dominant visual
per beat, move secondary elements away from text, and preserve quiet intervals.
Make the speaker centered in both front and side views. Use camera-aware crop
tracking; moving the entire canvas also moves captions and can reveal blank edges.

## Motion vocabulary

- Revenue: bundles/receipts accumulate; a graph reveals alongside the actual claim.
- Margin: isolate a fraction of the revenue, then show the resulting profit.
- Cash received: money lands in a wallet/account illustration on the spoken beat.
- Receivable: a document receives a pending/locked mark; cash remains unavailable.
- Paper profit: compare the document with usable cash, without fabricating balances.
- Scale: floors grow after a healthy foundation is established.
- Punchline: animate the meaningful words/objects; preserve the speaker's emphasis.

These are examples, not mandatory scenes. Use real numbers and screenshots for
proof; mark hypothetical numbers as an illustration/simulation. Animation should
explain the sentence through actions and transformations, not a sequence of
static text panels. A short clip need not use every available effect or engine.

Use charcoal as the main field, gray for structure, and yellow sparingly for
focus. Avoid rainbow/purple-pink styling, floating blur blobs, generic icon-card
triplets, constant bouncing and decorative UI/timecodes. Transition through an
object, camera movement or meaningful cut. Keep SFX selective and tied to action;
speech remains the primary audio.
